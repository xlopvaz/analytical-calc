Concentration Calculator

From instrument signal to concentration — calibration, dilution, LOD/LOQ, uncertainty.

How to use this calculator
  1. Pick a calibration type in the Calibration tab: external simple, internal standard, or standard addition.
  2. Enter your calibration points — type them in manually, or paste a table copied from Excel/CSV.
  3. Click "Calculate calibration" to get the fitted line, R², LOD and LOQ.
  4. Optionally save the calibration to History so you can reuse it without re-entering points.
  5. Go to the Samples tab, pick the calibration to use, and enter each sample's signal plus any dilution factors to get its concentration.
  6. Optionally enter a procedural/method blank signal in the Samples tab — it's automatically subtracted from every sample's result in that batch.
  7. For MC-ICP-MS, select that pill above the tabs to switch the Samples tab to replicate statistics and sample-standard bracketing (SSB) instead.
If the addition series was prepared from a pre-diluted aliquot, enter the factor(s) here (e.g. 10, 2) to refer the result back to the original sample.
